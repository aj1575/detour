# StayScout

Part of Detour, a group road-trip concierge built for Cal Hacks 13.0.

Ask where to stay and StayScout returns up to five hotels, inns, or campgrounds near your destination, with ratings and distance. In ASI:One they appear as a card. Everyone in the group chat can tap Vote for this. Yatra keeps one vote per person and shows the running tally. The Itinerary agent books the winner into the plan.

Try: where should we stay in Big Sur? · find a hotel near Carmel · campsites near Big Sur

Data: Google Places, searched around the trip's destination. Votes are stored in Convex.
