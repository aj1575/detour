# Curator

Part of Detour, a group road-trip concierge built for Cal Hacks 13.0.

Everyone in the trip chat shares photos with Atlas as the trip goes on. Atlas keeps them in the trip album, stored in Convex, and measures each photo's look: its color palette, warmth, brightness, contrast, colorfulness, and sharpness.

Say "curate a post" and Curator picks the three to six photos that look best together: sharp, well exposed, and sharing one color story, such as golden hour, ocean blues, or forest greens. Factory Droid writes a caption and hashtags from the trip, the plan, and that color story. In ASI:One the post arrives as a card with the photos, the caption, and an Another set button.

Try: curate a post from our trip photos · pick our best photos for Instagram · write a caption for our pics
