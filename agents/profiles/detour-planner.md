# Itinerary

Part of Detour, a group road-trip concierge built for Cal Hacks 13.0.

Say "plan our days" and Itinerary builds a one- to three-day plan for the trip Yatra set up: where you start, where you're going, your dates, and who's coming. It only uses real places. It pulls things to do and restaurants from Google Places, and puts the group's top-voted stay and activities first. Factory Droid writes the schedule with structured output. If Droid is unavailable, a plan is still built from the same places.

In ASI:One the plan arrives as a card with Find stays and Things to do buttons. It also shows live on the Detour trip board.

Try: plan our days · make an itinerary for the trip · what should we do each day?
